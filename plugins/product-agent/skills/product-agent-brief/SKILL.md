# product-agent-brief

Create the aligned source of truth for prototype, review, and delivery.

## Read first

1. `../product-agent-shared/references/language-and-localization.md`
2. `../product-agent-shared/references/product-agent-readiness.md`
3. `../product-agent-shared/references/product-agent-gotchas.md`
4. `../product-agent-shared/references/artifact-flow.md`

## Outputs

- One-sentence product judgment.
- Target user, scenario, problem, current substitute, and current loss.
- Goal, counter-metric, constraints, and non-fiction boundaries.
- Prototype scope or handoff scope.
- Status: draft, pending confirmation, or aligned.

## Gotchas

- Do not mark the brief aligned while material facts or scope decisions are missing.
- Do not ask to sync or deliver before the brief is aligned.
- Direction choice is not alignment.
