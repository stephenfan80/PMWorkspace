# product-agent-problem

Clarify whether the request maps to a real product problem.

## Read first

1. `../product-agent-shared/references/language-and-localization.md`
2. `../product-agent-shared/references/product-agent-workflow.md`
3. `../product-agent-shared/references/product-agent-gotchas.md`

## Inputs

- Product idea, feedback, screenshot, PRD note, or business goal.

## Outputs

- Current product judgment.
- Target user and trigger scene.
- Current substitute and current loss.
- Main goal, counter-metric, and evidence gaps.
- One next question or decision when needed.

## Gotchas

- A feature wish is not yet a problem definition.
- Do not rubber-stamp the idea; explain what could be wrong in product terms.
- Do not move to brief if the core user, substitute, loss, or goal is missing.
