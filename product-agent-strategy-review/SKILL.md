# product-agent-strategy-review

Challenge product scope and strategy before downstream work.

## Read first

1. `../product-agent-shared/references/language-and-localization.md`
2. `../product-agent-shared/references/product-agent-workflow.md`
3. `../product-agent-shared/references/product-agent-gotchas.md`

## Outputs

- Strategy conclusion.
- Main contradiction.
- Scope posture: expand, selectively expand, hold, shrink, or pivot.
- This-cycle validation.
- One decision when scope or promise changes.

## Gotchas

- Do not expand scope by default.
- If basic product facts are missing, return to `product-agent-problem`.
- A strategy change must be written back into the brief before prototype or handoff.
