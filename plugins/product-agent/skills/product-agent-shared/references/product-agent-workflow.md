# product_agent workflow

`product_agent` has four public stages.

## 1. 对齐

Clarify the product path, real problem, target user, current substitute, current loss, goal, counter-metric, constraints, and non-fiction boundaries.

Output: working card, product homework, pending questions, or aligned `product_brief`.

## 2. 原型

Use an aligned brief to create mobile-first prototype directions. One image equals one scheme and one screen task.

Output: `prototype_manifest` with scheme, screen, goal, counter-metric, and non-fiction boundaries.

## 3. 复审

Review whether prototype images faithfully represent the aligned product judgment.

Output: `prototype_review`, regenerate instructions, or decision items.

## 4. 交付

Turn the aligned brief and reviewed direction into a compact delivery contract.

Output: `handoff`, compact PRD, product design document, experiment notes, or explicit gaps.
